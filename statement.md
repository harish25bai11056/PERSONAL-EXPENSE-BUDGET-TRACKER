# Personal Expense & Budget Tracker

## Problem Statement

People often record expenses inconsistently and have limited visibility into how daily purchases affect monthly budgets. This project provides a reliable command-line application for recording expenses, setting category-based monthly limits, identifying budget breaches, and reviewing spending patterns.

## Scope of the Project

The system covers expense CRUD operations, category-based monthly budgets, threshold alerts, monthly and category analytics, input validation, and CSV persistence. It is designed for a single local user and does not include online accounts, banking integrations, shared budgets, or a graphical interface.

## Target Users

- University students managing personal living costs
- Individuals tracking recurring household expenses
- Anyone who needs a simple offline monthly budgeting tool

## High-level Features

- Add, view, edit, and delete expenses
- Categorize expenses and filter them by month or category
- Define monthly spending caps for each category
- Show alerts at 80% usage and when a cap is exceeded
- Generate monthly totals and category breakdowns
- Persist expenses and budgets in CSV files
- Validate amounts, dates, categories, IDs, and required text fields
